The honest answer

I am a co-parent and an iOS developer. When my own family changed shape, I went looking for an app to keep our schedule, expenses and messages in one place. Every app I found asked for my credit card before I could even see how it worked. Some wanted a subscription from both of us, which meant nothing worked unless my co-parent paid too.

So I built the app I was looking for.

Where most apps have costs, Kopare has my time

Co-parenting apps usually charge because they have offices, staff, investors and ad budgets to feed. Kopare has none of those. It is built and maintained by one developer who cares about this problem because I live it too. My cost is time, and I am glad to give it.

Free forever

Kopare is free for both parents, with everything included. The calendar, messaging, expense tracking, item tracking, all of it. No subscription, no trial that expires, no features behind a paywall.

And it will stay that way. Free forever is not a launch promotion. It is the reason Kopare exists.

Your data is yours

Kopare does not sell your data. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone, ever. Your family's schedule and messages are for your family.

Why give it away?

Because the weeks when I needed an app like this were hard enough without a paywall. If Kopare makes another family's handoffs calmer and their weeks easier to plan, that is the return I built it for.

If you have questions, ask me anytime. And if Kopare helps your family, a review on the App Store is the nicest thank you there is.

The developer behind Kopare Co-parent, iOS developer, one-person team

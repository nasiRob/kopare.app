Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 6, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Kopare ("the App," "we," "our") collects, uses, and protects your information. Data is only used for app purposes and never sold.

Information We Use

Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your email address, display name, and authentication details.

Calendar & Event Data: Events, custody requests, and messages you create are stored securely in our database (hosted on Firebase).

Device Information: We may collect non-identifying details such as device type, operating system, and app version to improve performance.

How We Use Information

  • To provide Kopare's core features: shared calendars, custody requests, and topic-based messaging.
  • To secure your account and sync data across devices.
  • To improve and maintain Kopare.

How We Share Information

With Co-Parents or Caregivers: Events, requests, and messages are shared only with members of your shared calendar.

With Service Providers: We use trusted third-party providers (such as Google Firebase) for hosting and data storage.

Never Sold to Advertisers: We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Data Retention

Events, requests, and messages remain available until deleted by you.

Account information is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account at any time.

Security

Kopare uses industry-standard security measures, ensuring that only authorized users can access shared calendars.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When changes are made, we will update the effective date above and provide notice where required.

Subscriptions and Payments

The app offers optional in-app subscriptions processed securely through Apple's App Store. Payment information is handled directly by Apple, and the app does not collect, store, or have access to users' payment or billing information.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact us through the app's support feature.
